NOTE ON BUNDLED GLYPH SETS
--------------------------
Symbols Nerd Font aggregates glyphs from several upstream icon sets, each of
which carries its own license — among them Font Awesome, Material Design Icons,
Octicons, Devicons, Codicons, Weather Icons, Powerline Extra Symbols, Font Logos
and Pomicons. The Nerd Fonts project does not embed each set's license in the
font file; authors, licenses and repositories are catalogued at
https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts and https://www.nerdfonts.com.
